Each fragment is one person-moment: situation (when/where), desire (the state they want), pain (what blocks them now), alternative (what they use or do now), failedAttempt (something they already tried that did not work, empty if none seen), believedCause (why THEY think it failed), hesitation (price, doubt or other reason they do not buy yet), quote (the original wording when available) and sourceIds. Use referenceGroups: each analysed reference represents a group of copy variants; a high variantCount means the competitor keeps pushing that message (a strong signal of what customers respond to), while a moment no group addresses is a gap worth a target. Mine competitor ads for the customer moments and hesitations they address (e.g. repeated doubts about authenticity, freshness or price); never carry a competitor's product claim into our facts. Then group similar fragments into targets (id t1.., label = situation + desire + pain in one line, experience tried_failed when the fragments show a failed attempt, otherwise first_time, fragmentIds). Prefer targets that are repeated across sources and include at least one that references do not already address when the data shows one. Score every target with fit: painStrength (1–5, how sharp and frequent the pain is in the customers' own words), productAnswer (1–5, how DIRECTLY and UNIQUELY our FACTS resolve that pain's real cause; a pain any product in the category answers equally scores 1–2), distinctness (1–5, how different the resulting message is from the supplied references), and a one-line reason. The concepts below must use the highest-scoring targets (score = product of the three). Use different targets for different concepts; only when there are fewer targets than concepts, reuse a target with a different entryPoint. entryPoint is what the ad puts first: pain (problem empathy), alternative (comparison with what they do now), doubt (quality proof), price (value or offer), desire (the outcome state). solutionPath explains HOW our product resolves that target's pain as a process, not a one-line result: for a tried_failed target use flow cause_reframe with stages past_attempt (what they tried) -> believed_cause (why they think it failed) -> real_cause (the actual cause, reframed) -> criteria (what to check instead) -> mechanism (how our product meets it) -> verification (what the viewer can check themselves) -> outcome (the state they get); for a first_time target use flow criteria_first with criteria -> mechanism -> verification -> outcome (optionally real_cause before criteria). Keep stages in that order. Each step has content, basis and sourceIds: mechanism and verification MUST be basis product_fact citing FACTS; real_cause and criteria may use general_knowledge (general category facts, never presented as our product's result) or reference/customer_voice; when FACTS do not show the product category is a registered functional food with an approved function, the reframe is about quality, authenticity or ease, never about health effects. PERSUASION CHAIN (chain, required, one per concept, the single message of that video): pain (① the customer's pain in their own words, basis customer_voice or reference with sourceIds) -> believedCause (② why they think it happens; content "" when the data shows none) -> realCause (③ the reframed actual cause; general_knowledge or product_fact; never a health effect unless FACTS show a registered functional food) -> requirement (④ what a solution must do to remove ③, written as a condition) -> productFact (⑤ the FACTS statement showing our product meets ④, basis product_fact with sourceIds) -> reasonWhy (⑥ WHY ⑤ is possible: the deeper FACTS behind it, e.g. what one capsule contains and how much; it must add a product detail that ⑤ does not already state — a number, amount, ingredient purity, process or manufacturer from FACTS; restating ⑤ is rejected; basis product_fact with sourceIds) -> outcome (the pain gone and the fragment's desire fulfilled, reusing the customer's own desire words; no invented emotions). STEP 3 funnel signals (Meta Andromeda: targeting stays broad and each creative must carry its own targeting signal). Treat decisionRole as the customer awareness stage: need_awareness = TOFU (does not know the product, often not even the exact need), comparison = MOFU (knows solutions exist, does not know why ours), final_decision = BOFU (knows the product, lacks a reason to decide now). Fill signals for every concept: avatarCallout describes a specific kind of person in a specific situation (never an invented personal name), not a broad category (e.g. "people whose snoring makes them feel sorry toward their partner", never just "people who snore"); painPoint names the concrete discomfort, preferably a fix they already tried that failed, phrased as a question when natural; mechanism states our unique way of working (unique_mechanism) or the real cause reframed (cause_reframe: "the problem is not X, it is Y, which our verified feature addresses") or real customer evidence (social_proof); a generic category claim ("X is the best solution") is not a mechanism because it sends shoppers to price comparison. offer: TOFU and MOFU must use type none with an empty statement, because a bare discount to people who do not know the product reads as spam. BOFU may call the brand by name and use an offer only when an offer source in FACTS states it, citing that offer source; prefer adding value (value_bundle such as 1+1 or bonus, free_trial) or risk_reversal (refund/guarantee) over a plain price cut, which loses margin and perceived value; urgency only when the offer source states a real deadline or limit. Never invent offers, guarantees, prices, deadlines or reviews. format: problem_empathy (TOFU: call the avatar, name the pain, show our unique way to solve it), mechanism_explainer (MOFU: card-news style visual of how ours works or the reframed cause), review_proof (MOFU or BOFU: real customer reviews/before-after, only when VOICES exist; before/after scenes are concept art and must not imply unverified results), benefit_offer (BOFU: the offer value up front), risk_reversal (BOFU: the refund/guarantee condition up front).
